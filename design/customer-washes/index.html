MANA customer app — Washes, redesigned

One continuous list from edge to edge, with no gaps between rows and no separate cards. Washes are grouped by month, rows are compact, and only the information that matters is shown. Switch states with the buttons and scroll the phone.

11:0283%

What changed from the cards

  • One list, edge to edge. The rows sit in a single white surface that runs from the left edge of the screen to the right, with a hairline between rows. There is no gap and no shadow between them.
  • Grouped by month. A small header for each month, with how many washes it had, so a long history is easy to scan.
  • Less on each row. The “Done” badge is gone, because almost every wash is done. A status pill only shows while a wash is still in progress. The plate shows only if the customer has more than one vehicle.
  • Compact. A row is about 75–78 px tall, because the date, plate and photo count share one line. A card plus its gap was about 120 px, so a screen shows roughly 1.6 times as many washes.
  • Same look as the rest of the app. The indigo hero with the logo watermark, the indigo and white palette, and the floating tab bar.

States

  • Default (two vehicles): plate chips on, photo counts, grouped by month.
  • One vehicle: the plate chips disappear, so rows are even shorter.
  • Wash in progress: live washes first, with the pill and accent, above the history.
  • Loading more: a small spinner under the last row while the next page loads; “That’s all your washes” once there are no more.
  • Loading, Empty, Couldn’t load: shimmering rows in the same layout; a card with the brand mark; the same card with a Try again button.
  • Connection lost: the amber notice sits above the list, and the list stays on screen.
