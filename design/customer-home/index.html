MANA customer app — Home, redesigned

The Home screen from your screenshot, redesigned in the new deep-indigo and white palette, with every state it can show. Switch states with the buttons, scroll the phone, and tap the tabs. Below the phone are the tokens, the component specs and an implementation map for the existing source files.

10:04

What changed, in short

  • One palette. Deep indigo and white from the new logo, with teal for "done" and a single warm gold reserved for rewards and "ready".
  • A real hero. Gradient with soft light, faint stars and the logo as a watermark, a clear greeting, and the branch as a glass chip. The first card overlaps it.
  • Cards, not hairline lists. Rounded white cards with soft indigo shadows, so each section reads as its own thing.
  • Free-wash cards show progress as drops. Each stamp is a water drop; the last slot is the gold reward.
  • Offers look like tickets, with the code ready to copy.
  • Floating bottom bar with an indigo pill behind the active icon.
  • Status-bar scrim. A fixed indigo fade at the very top keeps the clock and icons readable when content scrolls under them.

Colour tokens

Brand colours are the logo's. Neutrals are tinted indigo so greys never look flat against it. Teal and amber are kept, but only as meaning (done / reward).
