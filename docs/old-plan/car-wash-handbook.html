Your main plan: apartment communities

How it works: you go to apartment communities, gated communities and multi-floor buildings. The association gives you a place to wash. Residents book single washes or take a plan, and you wash at least 2 cars per visit (6–10 cars is much better). Doorstep washing for single houses comes after one year, so it isn't in this book yet.

Why the Aquaforce 1400 suits communities

  • Cordless: runs on a battery, so you don't need a power socket near each parking spot.
  • Takes water from a bucket or drum, so you don't need a tap at every spot.
  • 1400 PSI is enough for cars. Paint is safe at 1000–1500 PSI.
  • Portable: it comes apart and fits in a case, so it's easy to carry in lifts and parking areas.
  • Price: ₹37,999 offer price (MRP ₹51,350). Promec Aquaforce page

Ask Promec before you pay

  • How many cars per full charge, really? (The seller says "up to 3 hours". Most cordless washers last 20–45 minutes.)
  • Battery voltage and Ah, and charging time.
  • Water flow in litres per minute.
  • Price of a spare battery. For 10+ cars a day you'll probably need one.
  • Warranty, and the nearest service centre to Nellore.
  • Can the gun take a foam cannon?
  • Can they show it washing a real car (a demo) before you buy?

Get permission from the community first

  1. Meet the association secretary, president or facility manager. Take a printed price list and plan list.
  2. Agree on the wash spot: an open area with a drain is best. Use the basement only if they allow water there.
  3. Agree on the time: for example 6–11 am and 4–7 pm, when residents' cars are parked at home.
  4. Agree on water (can you use their tap? do you pay for it?) and on one power point for your vacuum.
  5. Register your workers with security (ID card copy, phone number).
  6. Get the permission in writing: a letter, an email or a WhatsApp message from the association.
  7. To say thank you, offer committee members a free first wash, or give the association a small discount on their cars.

Getting customers inside a community

  • Put a poster on the notice board and, with the association's OK, post a message in the residents' WhatsApp group.
  • Do a demo morning on a Saturday or Sunday: wash 2–3 cars in front of everyone.
  • Launch offer for the first month: ₹100 off the first wash, or a free tyre shine.
  • Keep a sign-up sheet for the plans. Plans are where communities bring you steady income.

Visit day routine

  1. Night before: fully charge the Aquaforce (and the spare battery). Wash and pack cloths by colour. Fill the MW bottles (see Mana Wash bottles and stickers).
  2. Load the kit: Aquaforce, water drum, 3 buckets with grit guards, Foam Pro 3.0 sprayer, mixed spray bottles, cloth boxes, brushes, vacuum and extension cable, and your phone with the software.
  3. Reach on time and set up only at the agreed spot. Put up a small board with your name and phone number.
  4. Wash the cars in the booked order using the same 15 steps.
  5. Leave the spot cleaner than you found it. No foam, mud or rubbish left behind. This keeps the association happy.
  6. Mark the washes in your software, collect payments (UPI is easiest), and confirm next week's bookings.
Water planningPlan about 40–60 litres per car (my estimate, including buckets). A 200 L drum covers about 4 cars. For 8–10 cars, carry 500 L or use the community tap. Confirm the Aquaforce's real water use with Promec.
Basement with no water allowedUse Wavex Rinseless Wash (1 L, ₹699): one bucket, wash mitt and drying towel, no rinsing. Use it only for cars with light dust. Muddy cars must go to the open wash spot.

Community sales system

  • Target map: list every apartment community in Nellore: name, area, approximate cars, association contact and status (not contacted / meeting / said yes / active). Keep it in your software.
  • Association pitch kit: printed brochure, price list and plans, insurance letter, worker ID and police verification list, before/after photos, and a permission letter for them to sign.
  • Resident ambassador: one happy resident in each community gets a free month for every 5 plan customers they bring.
  • Referral reward: ₹200 off next month for each friend who joins a plan.
  • Later: office and company car fleets as extra plan customers.
Community rules1. Minimum 2 cars per visit in one community. If only 1 car is booked, charge the normal price.
2. Fix one weekly day for each community (for example, Monday for Community A, Tuesday for Community B).
3. Customers book by WhatsApp before 8 pm the evening before.
4. You need a vehicle (car boot, van or small goods auto) to carry the drum and kit.
Later: branch roomsOpen a small branch room (staff base and kit store, no hub work) in an area only when: there are 8+ communities and about 150+ plan customers within about 3 km, the area's teams are full and travel from Kovur is eating time and fuel, and a trained Team Lead is ready to become Branch Manager.

Use the compressor in the wash

  • Drying: blow water out of mirrors, door handles, the grille, badges and the fuel lid. This stops drips after drying.
  • Interior: blow dust out of AC vents, seat rails and gaps before vacuuming.
  • Free tyre pressure check for every shop customer. It costs you nothing and customers remember it.
  • Rust and coating work: air sanders and spray guns.
  • Safety: use low pressure near paint and badges. Never point the air gun at a person.

The shop as the community team's base

  • Charge the Aquaforce batteries overnight at the shop.
  • Wash all cloths there every evening, using the Microfiber & Pad Washer.
  • Store the 20 L cans there. Fill the small bottles and the water drum before leaving.
  • Train new workers on shop cars before sending them to communities.
  • Hub staff always stay in the hub. They go to communities only in emergencies, when you say so (see Team, training and pay).

Turn community washes into shop jobs

  1. Car health card: after each community wash, tick what you noticed: rust, dull paint, scratches, yellow headlights, worn tyres, smelly interior. Save it in your software against that car, and send it to the customer on WhatsApp.
  2. Say one thing politely, for example: "Your door edges are starting to rust. We can fix it at our shop before it spreads."
  3. Community discount: 10% off shop work for residents of your communities.
  4. Pick-up and drop: your worker drives the car to the shop and back. For busy residents, this is the main reason to choose you.
  5. Nellore is close to the sea, and salty air rusts cars faster. Mention this politely when you suggest anti-rust.
Don't miss community slotsShop work must never pull workers away from a community booking. If you miss a slot, residents lose trust fast. Keep the community shifts fixed, and keep community teams separate from hub staff.
Track two incomes separatelyIn your software, mark every job as Community (with the community's name) or Shop. Each month you'll see which part earns more and where to put your time.

Graduation test (before moving to communities)

  • Knows the 15 steps, the cloth colour rules and the mixing chart by heart
  • Has washed 40+ practice cars with photo checks, averaging 4.5+ from you or the experienced person
  • Zero paint damage in the last 20 cars
  • Passes a short role-play: greets a "customer" politely, explains the package, asks for a rating
  • Can take before/after photos and record a wash in the software and on WhatsApp
  • Good attendance for 4 weeks (no unplanned leave)
  • You sign off

Cloth colour rules

Edgeless buffing towelSpray wax on paint only. The cleanest cloth you own.
Glass towelGlass and mirrors only. Nothing else.
Yellow cloth (350 GSM)Inside the car only: dashboard, doors, console, seats.
Grey cloth (350 GSM)Dirty jobs: wheels, tyres, door edges, engine, mats.
Golden rules1. If any cloth falls on the ground, it becomes a grey cloth from that moment. Never use it on paint again.
2. Never wash grey cloths together with paint cloths.
3. Never use the wash mitt on wheels.

Why few services can win a bigger market Every worker learns the same 15 steps, so training is fast and quality stays the same in every community · customers choose in seconds · you buy fewer products in bigger cans (cheaper) · teams can be copied again and again.
Reality check: this is a very hard plan To hit it you need: 24 communities with about 31 plan cars each by Month 12 · 9 community teams working two shifts (morning and evening) · about 29 local staff by Month 12, all trained through the hub · about 1 in 10 plan customers taking anti-rust each month · quality never dropping. What keeps this number steady

1

Renewals: keep plan customers coming back

About 1 in 10 plan customers leave every month (they move house, sell the car, or stop). To stay at the same size you must sell that many new plans every month (see the last column above).

  • UPI AutoPay for every monthly plan, so nobody forgets to pay.
  • Send the renewal message 3 days before a plan ends.
  • Call every customer who gives a rating below 4 the same day, and fix it free.
  • When someone cancels, ask why, and write the reason in the software.
  • Keep the resident ambassadors and the ₹200 referral reward running all the time, not just at launch.
  • Target: 9 out of 10 plans renew every month.
2

Staff: replace people quickly

  • The hub always trains 1–2 people, even when you aren't growing, so anyone who leaves is replaced within 2 weeks.
  • Keep the career ladder, bonuses and loyalty bonuses real. People who see a future stay longer.
  • Put a notice period (for example 15 days) in every worker agreement.
  • Never let a community slot go uncovered because someone left. Hub staff are the emergency backup.
3

Quality: one bad month can lose a whole community

  • Keep the average rating at 4.6 or higher.
  • Supervisors check 1 in 10 cars every day, and photo proof for every car continues.
  • Fix every complaint the same day. Residents talk in their WhatsApp groups, and bad news spreads faster than good.
  • Retrain any team whose rating drops below 4.5 for 2 weeks.
4

Communities: look after the associations

  • Meet or call each association once a month. Ask if there are any problems.
  • Festival goodwill: a free wash for committee members at Sankranti or Diwali.
  • Leave every wash spot cleaner than you found it, every single day.
  • Keep each community's permission letter up to date.
5

Equipment and stock: never stop work

  • Keep a spare Aquaforce (1 for every 4–5 teams) and spare parts at the hub.
  • Follow the reorder levels: order when 2 weeks of stock is left.
  • Budget about ₹5,000 per team every month for repairs, spare parts and replacing worn kit. It's already in the profit numbers above.
6

Seasons: smooth out the bad months

  • Rains (October–December): plans keep money coming in. Use the rain-day rule and push inside cleaning and anti-rust.
  • Summer (April–June): water can get short. Early slots (5:30–10 am), extra drums and Rinseless Wash.
  • Festivals (Sankranti, Ugadi, Dussehra, Diwali): extra slots for single washes.
7

Money: check the numbers every month

  • Review the numbers to track at the end of every month.
  • Keep yearly plan money in a separate account. Take only 1/12 a month.
  • Keep a 3-month reserve before taking extra profit out.
  • Once a year, review prices. Raise them a little for new customers only, if costs have gone up.
