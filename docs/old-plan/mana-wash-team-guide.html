Our promise and how we behave

The Mana Wash promise"On time, no scratches, or your next wash is free." Every one of us keeps this promise on every car.

How we look

  • Mana Wash T-shirt, cap and ID card, every day
  • Clean uniform, clean kit, MW bottles only
  • Put the small Mana Wash board at the wash spot

How we behave

  • Use the same greeting every time (below)
  • No phone use while washing, except photos and the software
  • No smoking, no loud music, no asking for tips
  • Never give customers your personal number. All bookings go through Mana Wash.
  • Respect residents and security staff
  • Leave every wash spot cleaner than you found it
GreetingStart: "Good morning sir/madam, I'm [your name] from Mana Wash. I'll start your car's [package] wash now. It will take about [time]."
Finish: "Your car is ready. Please check it, and rate us on WhatsApp. Thank you."

Everyone starts at the hub. You learn properly, pass the test, and then move to community work, where you earn a bonus on every car. Your base salary is as written in your offer letter.

Graduation test (to move to communities)

  • Know the 15 steps, the cloth colour rules and the mixing chart by heart
  • Wash 40+ practice cars with photo checks, averaging 4.5+
  • Zero paint damage in your last 20 cars
  • Pass a short role-play: greet a "customer", explain the package, ask for a rating
  • Take before/after photos and record a wash in the software and on WhatsApp
  • Good attendance for 4 weeks (no unplanned leave)
  • The owner signs off

Cloth colour rules

Each cloth has one job only. A cloth used on wheels has sand and brake dust in it. If it touches paint, it will scratch the car, and the customer will blame you.

Wash mittPaint only, with shampoo. Never wheels.
Drying towel (600 GSM)Drying paint and outside glass only.
Edgeless buffing towelSpray wax on paint only. The cleanest cloth you own.
Glass towelGlass and mirrors only. Nothing else.
Yellow cloth (350 GSM)Inside the car only: dashboard, doors, console, seats.
Grey cloth (350 GSM)Dirty jobs: wheels, tyres, door edges, engine, mats.
Golden rules1. If any cloth falls on the ground, it becomes a grey cloth from that moment. Never use it on paint again.
2. Never wash grey cloths together with paint cloths.
3. Never use the wash mitt on wheels.

Bottle rules 1. Use only the MW bottle with the right sticker. If a sticker is missing or unclear, don't use it. Tell your Team Lead.
2. Only the owner or supervisor fills bottles from the big cans.

Morning setup (before the first car)

  1. Night before: fully charge the Aquaforce battery (and the spare, if you have one).
  2. Fill the water drum, or confirm you can use the community tap. Check the Aquaforce hose, filter and gun for leaks.
  3. Check the ready-mixed spray bottles are full: MW Cleaner Strong, MW Cleaner Light, MW Glass (see the mixing chart).
  4. Fill Bucket 1 with 10 L water + 30 ml MW Shampoo. Fill Bucket 2 with 10 L clean water. Put a grit guard in both. Fill Bucket 3 (wheels) with clean water.
  5. Lay out clean cloths by colour on a clean shelf or table, never on the floor.
  6. Keep the MW Tyre Shine, MW Dash, MW Wax and MW Interior bottles ready with their applicators.
  7. Charge your phone for taking photos of each car.

The wash, step by step

Follow these steps in this exact order for every car. The coloured labels under each step show which packages include it: EXPRESS BASIC PREMIUM. Amounts are for a hatchback / sedan / SUV.

Two rules for the whole washWork in the shade: sun dries foam and shampoo on the paint and leaves marks. Always work from top to bottom: the roof is cleanest and the bottom is dirtiest.
In communitiesWherever a step says "pressure washer", use the Aquaforce. Make the foam with the hand foam sprayer. Rinse quickly to save battery and water. The steps are otherwise the same.

Part A: Receive the car

1

Welcome the customer and check the car

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
You need
Your phone
  1. Greet the customer. Ask which package they want and tell them the price and time.
  2. Walk around the car with the customer. Point to any scratch, dent or broken part.
  3. Take photos of all 4 sides, plus close-ups of any damage.
  4. Ask the customer to take out money, phones and valuables.
  5. Enter the customer's phone number, car number and package in your software (and attach the photos, if your software allows).

start washing before the photos. Without photos, an old scratch becomes your problem.

2

Park and prepare

2 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
  1. Park the car in the shade. Engine off, handbrake on.
  2. Close all windows and the sunroof fully.
  3. Touch the bonnet. If it is hot, wait 10 minutes or rinse it with water first.
  4. Take out the floor mats and keep them aside.

Part B: Outside wash

3

Clean the floor mats

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Cleaner Strong bottle (rubber mats only)
Brush
Tyre & Heavy Duty Brush
Cloth
Grey cloth
  1. Rubber mats: spray MW Cleaner Strong, scrub with the heavy-duty brush, rinse with the pressure washer, and stand them up to dry.
  2. Fabric mats: shake them hard and vacuum them later in Step 13.
4

Wheels and tyres (always before the body)

5–7 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Cleaner Strong bottle (ready mixed 1:5)
Amount
About 20 / 25 / 30 ml of cleaner concentrate per car
Brushes
Tyre & Heavy Duty Brush (tyre rubber), Wheel Cleaning Brush (rim face), Wheel Woolies (inside the rim)
Bucket
Bucket 3 (wheels only)
  1. Do one wheel at a time.
  2. Rinse the wheel with water.
  3. Spray MW Cleaner Strong on the tyre rubber, the rim and the wheel arch.
  4. Scrub the tyre rubber first with the heavy-duty brush. Brown foam means old dirt is coming off.
  5. Scrub the rim face and spokes with the Wheel Cleaning Brush.
  6. Put the Wheel Woolies through the spokes to clean inside the rim.
  7. Dip the brushes in Bucket 3 between wheels.
  8. Rinse the wheel fully with the pressure washer. Move to the next wheel.

let the cleaner dry on the rim in the sun, or use the wheel brushes on the paint.

5

Pre-rinse the whole car

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Tool
Aquaforce (or any pressure washer), plain water
  1. Hold the nozzle about 1–2 feet (30–60 cm) away from the paint.
  2. Rinse from the roof, then glass, then bonnet and boot, then sides, then the bottom.
  3. Spray well under the wheel arches and the bottom edges to remove mud.

go closer than 1 foot, or spray hard straight onto stickers, badges, rubber seals or peeling paint.

6

Snow foam

5 min + 3–5 min waiting
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Foam
Mix
100 ml MW Foam + 900 ml water in the foam sprayer bottle (1:9)
Amount
About 50 / 70 / 100 ml of MW Foam per car
Tool
hand foam sprayer (or a foam cannon, if your machine takes one)
  1. Pump the foam sprayer 20–30 times until it feels hard (or fix the foam cannon on the gun).
  2. Spray foam from the bottom of the car upward, so the foam stays longer on the lower, dirtier parts.
  3. Cover the whole car with thick foam, glass included.
  4. Wait 3–5 minutes. Clean the inside glass or prepare the next step meanwhile.
  5. If the foam starts drying, rinse it at once.

leave the foam until it dries, or foam in direct hot sun.

7

Rinse the foam

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
  1. Use the Aquaforce with its normal nozzle.
  2. Rinse from the top down: roof, glass, bonnet and boot, sides, bottom.
8

Two-bucket hand wash

8–12 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Shampoo, 30 ml in Bucket 1 (10 L water). For SUVs add 10 ml more.
Buckets
Bucket 1 = shampoo · Bucket 2 = clean water. Both with grit guards.
Cloth
Wash mitt
  1. Dip the mitt in Bucket 1 (shampoo).
  2. Wash one small area (half the roof, for example) in straight lines, not circles. Press softly. Let the mitt slide.
  3. Rub the mitt on the grit guard in Bucket 2 (clean water) to remove dirt.
  4. Go back to Bucket 1 for fresh shampoo, then the next area.
  5. Order: roof → glass → bonnet → boot → upper doors → lower doors → bumpers last.
  6. If the mitt falls on the floor, stop. Take a new clean mitt.
  7. Change the water in both buckets after every 3–4 cars, or sooner if it looks dirty.

wash in circles, press hard, or use a single bucket. These cause the fine scratches (swirl marks) customers hate.

9

Final rinse

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
  1. Rinse every bit of shampoo off, top to bottom.
  2. Rinse inside the fuel lid, mirrors, grille and badges, where shampoo hides.
  3. Do a last quick rinse of the wheels.
10

Dry the car

5–7 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Cloths
1 drying towel (600 GSM) for the body · 1 grey cloth for door edges and the boot edge
Product
MW Cleaner Light bottle (for door edges)
  1. Open the drying towel fully and lay it flat on the roof.
  2. Hold two corners and pull it slowly towards you. Don't rub. The towel drinks the water.
  3. Do the roof, glass, bonnet, boot, then the sides. Fold the towel when one side gets wet.
  4. Open each door. Spray a little MW Cleaner Light on the door edges and sills, then wipe with the grey cloth. Do the same for the boot edge and the fuel lid.
  5. Wipe the mirrors, handles and badges where water drips.
  6. At the shop: blow water out of mirrors, door handles, grille, badges and the fuel lid with the compressor air gun (low pressure), then wipe.

let the car dry by itself. It leaves white water spots, especially with hard borewell water.

Part C: Finishing

11

Glass and mirrors

4 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Glass bottle (ready mixed)
Amount
About 25 / 30 / 40 ml of mixed liquid per car
Cloths
2 glass towels: towel 1 to clean, towel 2 to polish dry
  1. Outside glass: spray on the glass, wipe with towel 1, then polish with dry towel 2 until there are no lines.
  2. Inside glass (Basic and above): spray on the towel, not on the glass, so it doesn't drip on the dashboard. Wipe, then polish with towel 2.
  3. Do the mirrors too. Lower each window 2 inches and clean the top edge.

clean glass in hot sun (it streaks), or use a yellow or grey cloth on glass.

12

Tyre shine

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Tyre Shine (use as is)
Amount
About 15 / 20 / 25 ml per car (all 4 tyres)
Applicator
Foam Sponge Applicator
Cloth
Grey cloth for mistakes
  1. Make sure the tyre is dry.
  2. Put a little MW Tyre Shine on the sponge, never straight onto the tyre.
  3. Spread it evenly on the side wall of the tyre only.
  4. If any goes on the rim or paint, wipe it off at once with the grey cloth.

put tyre shine on the part that touches the road. It's slippery and dangerous, especially on bikes.

13

Vacuum the inside

6–10 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Tool
Wet and dry vacuum · Horse Hair Brush for gaps
  1. Take out any rubbish (ask the customer first if unsure).
  2. Vacuum from top to bottom: seats first, then the floor.
  3. Push the seats fully forward, vacuum behind; push them back, vacuum in front.
  4. Use the horse hair brush to sweep dust out of gaps while vacuuming.
  5. Vacuum the boot and the fabric mats.
  6. At the shop: before vacuuming, blow dust out of the seat rails and gaps (and the AC vents for Premium) with the compressor air gun, with the doors open.
14

Dashboard and inside plastics

4–5 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
Product
MW Dash (use as is)
Amount
About 10 / 15 / 20 ml per car
Cloth
Yellow cloth or Yellow Microfiber Applicator Sponge
Brush
Premium only: Horse Hair Brush for AC vents and buttons
  1. First wipe off dust with a dry yellow cloth.
  2. Spray MW Dash on the applicator or cloth, never on the dashboard.
  3. Wipe the dashboard, centre console, door panels and door pockets.
  4. Premium only: brush the AC vents and buttons with the horse hair brush.
  5. Wipe once more with a dry part of the cloth so it isn't oily.

put MW Dash on the steering wheel, pedals or gear knob (they become slippery), or on any screen or glass.

15

Final check and hand over

3 min
EXPRESSBASICPREMIUM
  1. Put the mats back, dry side down.
  2. Walk around once more. Check for water drips, foam left in gaps, glass streaks and tyre shine on the rims.
  3. Check the inside for missed dust, and anything the customer left behind.
  4. Take 2–3 "after" photos (good for WhatsApp status and Instagram).
  5. Show the car to the customer, collect payment, and thank them. Ask them to come back in 7–10 days.

Premium extra steps

For the PREMIUM package (and the Premium wash in the Shine Plan), add these two jobs to the normal steps.

P1

Spray wax (do it right after Step 10, drying)

8–12 min
Product
MW Wax (use as is; works on a wet or dry car)
Amount
About 20 / 25 / 35 ml per car
Cloths
2 edgeless buffing towels: towel 1 spreads, towel 2 buffs to a shine
  1. Work on one panel at a time (bonnet, then roof, then one door).
  2. Spray 2–3 times on the panel.
  3. Spread it evenly with towel 1 in straight lines.
  4. Turn to towel 2 (dry) and buff until it shines and no lines are left.
  5. Move to the next panel. Skip the glass and the black rough plastic.

spray too much. More wax doesn't mean more shine, it only leaves greasy lines.

P2

Interior plastic and leather cleaning (do it before Step 14)

12–15 min
Product
MW Interior (use as is)
Amount
About 15 / 20 / 25 ml per car
Cloth
2 yellow cloths
Brush
Horse Hair Brush
  1. Spray MW Interior on the brush or cloth.
  2. Clean the dashboard, steering wheel, gear knob, console, door panels and door handles.
  3. For leather or rexine seats: spray on the horse hair brush, scrub gently in small circles, then wipe with a yellow cloth.
  4. Wipe everything dry with the second yellow cloth.
  5. Then do Step 14 (MW Dash) for the shine, but still not on the steering wheel or pedals.

After each car and end of day

After every car

  • Put used cloths in separate laundry buckets: one for grey cloths, one for everything else.
  • Rinse the wash mitt in clean water.
  • Rinse the wheel brushes in Bucket 3.
  • Check the shampoo bucket. Change the water every 3–4 cars.
  • Refill the foam cannon bottle if it's low.
  • Enter the car in your software: car number, package, price paid, payment method.

End of the day

  • Wash cloths in the washing machine with the microfiber detergent (follow the label amount). Wash grey cloths in a separate load.
  • No fabric softener, no bleach, no ironing. They ruin microfiber.
  • Dry cloths in the shade. Fold and store them in closed boxes by colour.
  • Empty and wash all 3 buckets and grit guards.
  • Run clean water through the foam cannon so it doesn't block.
  • Close every chemical can tightly. Keep them out of the sun and away from children.
  • Count the cash and UPI and match them with today's total in your software.

Photos, ratings and damage

Photos for every car

  • Before: 4 photos (front, back, both sides) plus close-ups of any old damage.
  • After: 4 photos of the outside, plus 1 of the inside for Basic and Premium.
  • Save them in the software against the car. No photo, no bonus.

Ratings

  • After every car, the customer gets a WhatsApp message asking for a rating from 1 to 5.
  • Your team's monthly average decides your bonus.
  • A rating below 4 means the owner or supervisor calls the customer, and the team fixes the problem free the same day.
If a car gets damaged 1. Stop and tell your Team Lead or the owner at once. Never hide it.
2. Take clear photos of the damage.
3. Don't argue with the customer. The owner or supervisor will talk to them.
4. Hiding damage is the most serious mistake you can make at Mana Wash. Telling the truth at once is always better.

Safety

Chemicals

  • Wear gloves when using MW Cleaner Strong.
  • In the eyes: wash with clean running water for 15 minutes, then tell the owner and see a doctor.
  • On skin: wash with soap and water.
  • Swallowed: don't vomit. Tell the owner at once and go to a doctor with the bottle.

Machines and power

  • Never point the Aquaforce, the pressure washer or the compressor air gun at a person.
  • Keep extension cables and plugs dry and away from water.
  • Charge batteries only at the hub, on the proper charger.
  • Lift full water drums with two people, or move them on the trolley.
  • Never wash a car with the engine running or a hot bonnet.
