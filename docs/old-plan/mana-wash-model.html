Mana Wash Business Model

Sreenivasulu owns the Mana Wash brand and asked Sprixia to build its software. He runs his own branches and sells Mana Wash franchises, with the software included as a monthly package. Franchise owners pay Mana Wash. Mana Wash pays Sprixia for building and running the software.

The two agreements

Sprixia ↔ Mana Wash (Sreenivasulu)

Software agreement

  • Sprixia builds, runs and updates the Mana Wash software
  • Mana Wash pays Sprixia: build fee, monthly running and support
  • Clear on who owns the software and the data
  • Clear on response time for fixes and new features
Mana Wash ↔ franchise owner

Franchise agreement

  • Use of the Mana Wash name, menu and model
  • Franchise fee + monthly package (brand + software)
  • Must follow HQ prices, services and quality rules
  • Area given to the branch, length of the agreement, exit rules

How the money flows

CustomersPay the branch for washes, plans and add-ons
Franchise ownersKeep their branch income and pay Mana Wash the franchise fee and monthly package
Mana Wash (Sreenivasulu)Earns from his own branches and from every franchise
SprixiaPaid by Mana Wash to build and run the software

More franchises → more income for Mana Wash → a bigger, steadier software business for Sprixia. Everyone wins when Mana Wash grows.
