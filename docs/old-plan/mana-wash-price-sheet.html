How these numbers are worked out

  • Supplies = all liquids + cloth and tool wear, from the handbook's Supplies section (my estimates; update them after your test).
  • Labour = the team's base salaries and bonuses, spread over about 400 washes a month per team. Bigger cars take longer, so they cost more labour.
  • Fuel = about ₹20 per community wash when a team is busy.
  • Not included: water, electricity, rent, booking staff, marketing, vehicle EMI and your own salary.
  • GST: not charged until you register. After registration, prices stay the same and about 15% of each price goes to GST.
