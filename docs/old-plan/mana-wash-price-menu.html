Good to know
  • All prices are final. No extra charges.
  • Community price applies when 2 or more cars are washed in the same community on the same visit.
  • Plans: one car per plan, maximum 1 wash a week. Unused washes end with the month. Bonus washes and the free month must be used within the plan.
  • Rain on your booked day? Your wash moves to the next dry day.
  • Very muddy cars (after a trip or off-road): ₹100–200 extra.
  • Second car in the same home: 10% off its plan.
