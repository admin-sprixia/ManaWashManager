# MANA Car Wash (customer app) – all features

## Signing in
- Sign in with your mobile number.
- A 6-digit code comes on WhatsApp; the app checks it as soon as all 6 digits are in.
- You can ask for a new code after a short wait.
- The app remembers your number for next time.
- One account works at every MANA branch you visit.

## New customers (number not known yet)
- "Car wash at your home" page for people who are not customers yet.
- Two choices: **Find my place** (join a place we already wash at) or **Call me back**.
- Short "Call me back" form:
  - Share your current location, or pick your area (Kavali, Kovur, Nellore…).
  - The app tells you if a MANA branch washes in your area.
  - Say how many cars and bikes.
  - Optional: kind of place (apartment, house, small building, office, other), place name, address, your name, best time, notes.
  - Outside our areas you can still send it; we tell you when MANA starts near you.
- See your request status ("Request sent").
- Change the request details or cancel the request.
- "We now wash at a place near you" banner with a **Join now** button when the team starts a place near you.
- Use a different number.

## Wash at your home (places and plans)

### Finding your place
- Places are apartments, colonies or streets where the team washes on fixed days.
- See places near you (share your location), nearest first, with photos.
- Type the place code from the QR poster at your gate (for example K7M2QPX).
- Scan the QR poster with your phone camera, or open a neighbour's link; the app opens right on that place.
- A link opened before signing in waits and opens after you sign in.
- Place not listed? Ask for a call back, or call your branch.

### Joining a place
- See the place photo, wash days and time, and the branch.
- Pick your flat or house (by block and number).
- Optional: where your vehicle is parked (for example "Basement, slot 14").
- Add one or more vehicles (up to 6 at once): your saved vehicles, or just pick the vehicle type.
- Each vehicle gets its own choice:
  - **Plans** (monthly, quarterly, yearly) with "Most popular" and "Save X%" tags.
  - **One wash** on the place's next wash day; pick one or more services.
- Pay in full, or pay in parts (a first part now, the rest later).
- Summary before joining: total, first wash day and pay-by time.
- After joining:
  - "You're in!", or "Almost done!" if a payment is still needed.
  - Pay button for each vehicle that needs payment.
  - Place full? You go on the waiting list and are told.
  - Some places need the team to confirm you first; the app says so.

### Paying (Razorpay)
- Pay online only: UPI, card, net banking through Razorpay.
- The first payment is made before the first wash.
- The app waits and shows the result on its own:
  - Paid: "Payment received", next wash day, See receipt.
  - Failed or expired: "No money was taken", Try again.
  - Still waiting: open the payment page again, or pay later.
- The team can also send you a payment link on WhatsApp; it opens the same page.

### Receipts
- A GST receipt for every payment, with a receipt number.
- Shows what you paid for, GST, total and any refunds.
- Share the receipt as text.

### My place
- Shown on Home as a "My place" card (or a "Wash at your home" card if you haven't joined one).
- The Home card tells you what needs you: waiting for the team, payment due, plan ending soon, or the next wash day.
- Your flat, next wash day and all your vehicles with their plan status.
- Add another vehicle.
- Call the branch.
- Invite neighbours (share the place link).
- "I moved out": leave the place; open plans are closed and refunded as per the rules.

### Managing a plan
- Washes used and washes left, with a progress bar.
- Next wash days.
- Skip a wash (before the cut-off time); undo a skip. Skipped washes don't use up your plan.
- Pause for a holiday: pick the start day and number of days; end a pause early.
- See payment parts (paid, due, late) and pay the next part.
- All receipts for the plan.
- Renewal reminder 7 days before the plan ends; renew in one tap and pay. Nothing is charged automatically.
- Cancel a plan or a one-time wash: unused washes are refunded to how you paid, in 5–7 days.

## Home
- Greeting with your name ("Good morning, Rohit").
- Your branch shown at the top.
- My place card (see above).
- Live wash card: shows when your vehicle is being washed and when it is ready.
- Latest wash: service, date, price, number plate, status.
- Free-wash cards: stamps for each wash, "1 more wash and the next one is free".
- Gold badge when a free wash is ready.
- Offers waiting for you (like "7% off your next wash") with a code you can copy.
- Gifts the car wash still owes you.
- Invite a friend shortcut.
- Help & support shortcut.

## My washes
- Every wash you have had, at every branch.
- Washes in progress shown first as "Today".
- Older washes grouped by month, newest first; more load as you scroll.
- Wash details:
  - Service(s), total and branch.
  - How you paid (cash, UPI, etc.).
  - Before / after photos; tap for full screen and swipe between them.
  - Rate the wash with 1–5 stars and an optional comment.
  - You can change your rating for 14 days.
  - Happy ratings get a "Review us on Google" button.
  - Report a problem with this wash, and see your reports for it.

## My vehicles
- All your vehicles with their number plates, make and model.
- Number of washes and last wash date for each.
- Free-wash cards, offers and gifts shown under each vehicle.
- Add a vehicle:
  - Pick the make from a list with logos (search works).
  - Pick the model (search works).
  - The vehicle type (hatchback, sedan, SUV, bike, scooter…) is picked for you from the model; you can change it.
  - Type the registration number.
  - The team checks it before it shows up.
- See vehicles waiting for the team, and cancel them.
- Remove a vehicle from the app (the car wash keeps its history).

## Visit us
- Every branch with:
  - Open now / closed and closing time.
  - Doorstep service note ("We also wash at your door") when the branch offers it.
  - Address; tap for directions in maps.
  - Opening hours and weekly off.
  - Phone number with Call and WhatsApp buttons.
- Price list for each branch:
  - Pick your vehicle from photos.
  - See every service with its price for that vehicle.
  - Switch between "At the shop" and "At your door" prices (when the branch does both).
- Your own branches are shown first.

## Invite friends
- Ready-made invite message with your number.
- Share on WhatsApp or any other app.
- Your friend gets 5–10% off their first wash; you get 5–10% off your next one.
- See the friends you invited and whether they visited.

## Help & support
- Branch details, directions, hours, Call and WhatsApp buttons.
- Report a problem (about a bill, the staff or anything else).
- Choose what it is about and write what happened.
- See all your reports, their status ("Team is looking", sorted) and the team's reply.

## Profile
- Your name and number; edit your name.
- Links to Invite friends, Help & support and your branch.
- Sign out.
- Sign out on all phones (if you lost a phone or shared your code).
- Delete your app account (your washes stay with the car wash).
- App version and support email.

## Everywhere in the app
- Bottom tabs: Home, Washes, Vehicles, Visit us, Profile.
- Pull down to refresh.
- Loading placeholders while data comes in.
- Clear messages when there is no internet, with "Try again".
- Big, easy layout on tablets.
